**How to Play****Thank you for playing this game!****\[About 90% of this game was made with the help of AI.]****Controls****1. Flick sideways to move. Flick in any of 3 vertical directions to hit the ball.****2. Hit a can with the ball for +5 sec on the timer (+6 sec at 30+ combo).****Score****Where you hit                       Forehand   Backhand****Wall                                100        110****Special ball (under 20 combo)       350        385****Special ball (20-29 combo)          420        462****Special ball (30+ combo)            525        578**
